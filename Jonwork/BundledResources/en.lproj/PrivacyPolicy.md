# Jonwork Privacy Policy

## Overview

Jonwork is a native client for conversations in your connected workspace. This policy describes what the app itself stores and when information leaves your device.

## Account and Connection Information

Workspace sign-in may process your account email under the workspace service’s policy. The app sends a client connection identifier with session commands to enforce controller ownership. This build does not provide native email/password sign-in or send a separate installation identifier. Credentials are kept in Keychain, not in public support reports.

## AI Sharing Permission

Before sending messages or attachments, Jonwork asks permission to share conversation history, attachments, and tool results with the displayed workspace and AI provider. Permission applies to that connection, workspace, and model. You can revoke it in Settings; future sends and retries will ask again. Revocation does not erase data previously sent. Ask your workspace administrator about retention, data deletion, and any external provider’s policy.

## Information Stored on Your Device

- Jonwork stores app preferences and local conversation metadata needed to present and resume your work.
- Workspace credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain.
- Jonwork does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

## Connected Workspace Processing

- Messages, attachments, tool requests, and conversation controls you submit are sent to the workspace service you connect to so it can run the requested work.
- Conversation sessions and generated results may be retained by that workspace service. Retention and access are controlled by the operator of the connected workspace.
- If a workspace uses external model or tool providers, relevant conversation data may be processed under those providers' policies.

## Apple Services

If you choose voice dictation, audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service according to Apple's privacy policy.

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy as Jonwork changes. The current version will be available in the app.

## Contact Us

For app support, visit [Jonwork Support](https://github.com/xiz2680/jonwork-ios/issues). GitHub issues are public: never include passwords, tokens, private conversations, attachments, or personal account information. For private account or data requests, contact your workspace administrator.
